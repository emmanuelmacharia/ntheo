# The viewer sits on light, not black

The immersive viewer and the recap player both started on a near-black sheet, the convention most lightboxes follow. Against this archive it read as a separate application dropped on top of the site, and the darkness competed with the photographs rather than receding behind them.

Both now sit on the same warm paper as the rest of the gallery, with the current image blown up, blurred hard and laid underneath at low opacity. Each photo supplies its own backdrop, so the surround shifts as you page and the colour always belongs to what you are looking at. The blur source is the stored LQIP, which is already in the payload, so the effect costs no extra bytes.

Videos get the same treatment rather than a player chrome of their own: full size, native controls, one obvious way back.

## Consequences

Controls sit on translucent white pills instead of relying on a dark field for contrast.
